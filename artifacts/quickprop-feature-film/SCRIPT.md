# QuickProp — 10 Second Feature Film

## Director's treatment

- **Promise:** QuickProp turns the agent's whole listing workflow — capture, manage, match, and share — into one fast mobile experience.
- **Identity:** 16:9 landscape. Official QuickProp green `#2C6D5D`, deep ink `#0B1929`, clean white, and pale mint. Plus Jakarta Sans carries both display and supporting copy.
- **Hero:** A large, recognizable mobile app interface that transforms from an agent dashboard into a property listing and then a branded share card.
- **Motion:** Camera push-throughs across interface planes, masked kinetic type, and shape-matched green apertures.
- **Arc:** Immediate app-first hook, fast workflow acceleration, a short readable share payoff, then a calm official brand close.
- **Payoff:** The final listing card collapses into the official QuickProp wordmark on a clean white field, structurally quieter than the feature montage.
- **Brand assets:** Official logo downloaded from `https://www.quickprop.melios.co.zw/logo.png` to `attached_assets/quickprop-logo.png`; verified PNG, 175×47 RGBA.
- **Sound direction:** One upbeat, polished electronic instrumental at roughly 112 BPM; crisp pulse, warm synth bass, no vocals. Beat alignment remains unverified until the music exists.

## Full shot script

### Shot 01 / 0.00–2.20s / 2.20s — “YOUR PORTFOLIO. MOVING.”

**Purpose:** Establish within the first second that QuickProp is a mobile command centre for property agents.

**Exact content:** “YOUR PORTFOLIO.” appears at 0.18s; “MOVING.” replaces the lower line at 0.58s. Inside the phone: “Welcome back, Tendai”, “Today’s Overview”, “12 Active Listings”, “8 Active Leads”, “New Listing”, and “Share Brochure”. All interface text remains visual artwork, not interactive controls.

**Composition and artistic idea:** Deep ink field with a faint property-grid line system. A phone frame occupies roughly 78% of the frame’s short edge, cropped on the lower-right and tilted 5°. The type owns the left 42% with generous safe-frame inset. The artistic idea is that the word “MOVING.” physically drives the interface: its green full stop becomes a status dot inside the phone.

**Timed choreography:** 0.00–0.18s: a large green dot is already visible near centre, eliminating an empty first frame; it snaps to the phone’s notification position while the phone resolves from 1.35× scale and 8px blur. 0.18–0.60s: “YOUR PORTFOLIO.” unmasks upward in two word groups. 0.42–0.95s: dashboard stat cards snap in with stiff 90ms staggering. 0.58–1.05s: “MOVING.” punches in at 10vmin, tracking compressing to rest. 1.10–1.55s: “New Listing” highlights green; 1.55–1.82s: “Share Brochure” answers with a brief mint pulse. 1.82–2.20s: the green quick-action tile scales toward camera to 3.1×, the phone tilts toward centre, and headline slices blur outward for the handoff.

**Transition contract:** The green “New Listing” tile is the physical carrier. Its rounded rectangle expands through the lens while the next shot’s listing plane begins resolving beneath it before the tile fully fades.

**Assets and technique:** Programmatic phone/interface artwork based on the existing QuickProp Agent dashboard; Lucide icons; CSS grid; Framer Motion scale, mask, and blur.

**Sound intent:** A clean opening pulse at 0.00s, two light percussive ticks under the stat cards, and a stronger lift into 2.20s if supported by the generated instrumental.

### Shot 02 / 2.20–5.20s / 3.00s — “LIST. MATCH. MOVE.”

**Purpose:** Show the core workflow: a listing becomes structured inventory and immediately produces a useful buyer match.

**Exact content:** “LIST.” at 2.28s, “MATCH.” at 2.78s, “MOVE.” at 3.28s. Listing artwork reads “Borrowdale”, “US$285,000”, “4 beds”, “3 baths”, “Borehole”, and “3 buyer matches”. A small label reads “PUBLISHED”.

**Composition and artistic idea:** The green transition field splits open diagonally to reveal a deep-ink world. A large angled listing plane fills 70% of the short edge from centre-right, with a stylized architectural photo area, data rows, and a bright match badge. Oversized outlined words sit behind the plane, cropped by frame edges. The artistic idea is a listing card behaving like a folding property dossier: its sections physically unfold as information becomes useful.

**Timed choreography:** 0.00–0.35s relative: “LIST.” fires within 50ms of the wipe completing; the listing plane settles from 1.5× and 10px blur. 0.35–0.85s: image panel unfolds from the card’s top edge; price rolls up into place. 0.58s: “MATCH.” replaces “LIST.” behind the card. 0.85–1.35s: bed, bath, and borehole chips trace on in sequence. 1.10–1.65s: a path line sweeps from the listing to the buyer-match badge; badge counts 1 → 2 → 3 with three discrete snaps. 1.08s: “MOVE.” replaces “MATCH.” and remains faintly outlined behind the card. 1.72–2.35s: “PUBLISHED” stamps onto the top edge and the whole plane rotates from -4° to 1°, revealing depth. 2.35–3.00s: the photo panel pushes toward centre while the data layers shear away; the match badge remains attached until the incoming share card is visible.

**Transition contract:** The listing’s image panel and match badge carry forward. The panel expands into the brochure image area; the circular badge becomes the share scene’s green aperture ring. Scale-through and diagonal split overlap for 300ms.

**Assets and technique:** Programmatic listing artwork and abstract architectural image made from layered geometric planes; SVG path trace; Framer Motion perspective, clip path, counters driven by `useSceneTimer`.

**Sound intent:** Three compact rhythmic hits for LIST / MATCH / MOVE, then a rising tonal accent into the share reveal. Alignment remains unverified.

### Shot 03 / 5.20–7.80s / 2.60s — “SHARE BEAUTIFULLY.”

**Purpose:** Make branded property marketing feel fast, finished, and ready for real channels.

**Exact content:** “SHARE” appears at 5.35s and “BEAUTIFULLY.” at 5.72s. The share card reads “Borrowdale Residence”, “US$285,000”, “QuickProp”, and “Ready to share”. Channel labels: “WhatsApp”, “Facebook”, and “LinkedIn”.

**Composition and artistic idea:** A pale-mint circular aperture opens from the carried match badge. A tall brochure card is centred and occupies 82% of the short edge, with a green brand bar, image crop, price, and concise details. Channel chips orbit outside the card without crossing the headline zone. The artistic idea is that the raw listing folds into polished marketing: card panels align, the green bar locks in, and the channel chips receive the finished asset.

**Timed choreography:** 0.00–0.40s relative: the aperture expands while the share card enters already 65% visible, settling from 1.42× to 1.00. 0.15–0.65s: “SHARE” unmasks above the card; “BEAUTIFULLY.” follows below at 0.52s. 0.55–1.15s: image, details, and green brand bar fold into one card in three distinct beats. 1.05–1.65s: WhatsApp, Facebook, and LinkedIn chips arrive one at a time along a traced arc. 1.55–2.05s: “Ready to share” checks on; a light sweep crosses only the green brand bar. 2.05–2.60s: the card holds readable for 180ms, then the green brand bar expands vertically while the channel chips are pulled into its edges, beginning the finale before the scene ends.

**Transition contract:** The brochure’s green brand bar is the carrier. It expands to cover the frame while “BEAUTIFULLY.” compresses into the first finale word; the official logo is not yet visible.

**Assets and technique:** Programmatic brochure artwork, Lucide channel-neutral icons, SVG orbit path, clip masks, and scale transforms.

**Sound intent:** Bright upward accent as the card assembles, three subtle ticks for channels, then a clean downbeat as the green bar fills the frame.

### Shot 04 / 7.80–10.00s / 2.20s — “LIST. CONNECT. CLOSE.”

**Purpose:** Resolve the feature story into a concise brand promise and a clean official lockup.

**Exact content:** “LIST.” at 7.82s, “CONNECT.” at 8.12s, “CLOSE.” at 8.44s. At 8.78s the official QuickProp logo appears. Supporting line: “Property, moving forward.” at 9.12s. No CTA.

**Composition and artistic idea:** Start on a full QuickProp-green field with one oversized white word at a time, each owning the frame and cropping slightly at the horizontal edges. At 8.72s, a white radial aperture opens from the “O” in “CLOSE.” and reveals a clean white field. The official logo is centred at large readable scale with a thin mint rule beneath. The artistic idea is a scale reversal: frenetic full-frame feature words collapse into a quiet, authoritative brand signature.

**Timed choreography:** 0.00–0.28s relative: “LIST.” lands immediately, then compresses backward. 0.28–0.62s: “CONNECT.” replaces it through overlapping blur/scale. 0.62–0.96s: “CLOSE.” punches in; its “O” holds as the other letters shear away. 0.92–1.28s: the “O” becomes a white aperture, opening to reveal the official logo with a subtle 1.12× to 1.00 settle. 1.28–1.62s: the mint rule draws left-to-right and “Property, moving forward.” fades up. 1.62–1.92s: resolved readable lockup. 1.92–2.20s: the rule continues left beyond the logo and curls into a small green dot at the same screen position used in Shot 01, creating the loop handoff without removing the logo before the final frame.

**Transition contract:** End-to-start continuity uses the mint rule’s leading green dot. On loop, it becomes Shot 01’s notification/status dot while the white field is replaced by deep ink behind the incoming phone.

**Assets and technique:** Official verified PNG logo via `@assets/quickprop-logo.png`; kinetic typography; radial clip path; SVG rule.

**Sound intent:** Three concise word hits followed by a warm resolved chord under the logo. The chord should end cleanly at 10.00s without a long tail.

## Variety and floor review

- **Distinct shots:** Dashboard is asymmetric and device-led; listing is a dark perspective dossier; sharing is centred and aperture-led; finale is full-frame kinetic type resolving to a minimal lockup.
- **Substantial motion:** Every shot transforms the product state after its entrance, and each final 15–20% begins the next handoff.
- **Beat density:** Opener changes at least every 0.4–0.5s; all later shots contain visible changes at least every 0.8s.
- **Hero scale:** Phone, listing plane, and brochure card each occupy 70–82% of the short edge.
- **Type scale:** Hero words use 10–30vmin; supporting copy uses at least 2.8vmin; safe insets remain 4–6%.
- **Zone ownership:** Shot 01 reserves left for headline and right for phone. Shot 02 puts outline type behind the card and the badge upper-right. Shot 03 gives headline top/bottom and card centre; chips orbit outside those zones. Shot 04 is intentionally single-focus.
- **No empty frame or wipe tail:** Every incoming shot begins with its carrier visible; the first finale word fires as the green wipe completes.
- **Cohesion:** Green, ink, white, mint, one type family, rounded cards, and shared scale-through geometry connect all four compositions.
- **Feasibility:** All visuals can be rendered with React, CSS, SVG, Framer Motion, the official logo, and existing icon packages. No generated imagery is required because the subject is software UI rather than a physical product.
