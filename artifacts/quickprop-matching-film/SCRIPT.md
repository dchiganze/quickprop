# QuickProp — Property Matching Film

## Director's treatment
- **Promise:** QuickProp makes buyer intent visible, comparable, and actionable for property agents.
- **Identity:** QuickProp green `#2C6D5D`, warm porcelain, ink, and citrus-lime match accents; Plus Jakarta Sans throughout, with tabular figures as the data voice.
- **Hero:** A buyer preference profile that resolves into a property match card, then expands into the living Matches workflow.
- **Motion:** Criteria orbit and snap-fit; card-to-card match cut; radial score reveal with a fast camera push.
- **Arc:** A quiet signal becomes a precise match, then a clear collaboration state and confident brand close.
- **Payoff:** The final lockup replaces the busy matching system with one calm green field and the official logo.
- **Format/runtime:** 16:9 landscape, 10 seconds.
