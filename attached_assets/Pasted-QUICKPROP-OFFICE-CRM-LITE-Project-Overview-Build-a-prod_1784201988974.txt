QUICKPROP OFFICE (CRM LITE)

Project Overview

Build a production-ready web application called QuickProp Office.

QuickProp Office is a lightweight property agency operating system.

It is NOT an enterprise CRM.

Its purpose is to help agencies:

• Manage every property mandate

• Search their entire inventory instantly

• Track buyers

• Track leads

• Collaborate between agents

• Generate brochures

• Market properties

• Publish inventory to QuickProp

The system must be modern, fast and incredibly simple.

The design philosophy is:

Every mandate belongs in the system.

Whether or not it is publicly advertised.

SUCCESS CRITERIA

The platform should allow an agency to:

• Store every property mandate regardless of whether it is advertised.

• Instantly search thousands of properties.

• Track every buyer and seller.

• Manage leads efficiently.

• Generate professional marketing material in one click.

• Publish or unpublish listings with a single action.

• Collaborate across multiple branches.

• Scale from a 2-person agency to a 500-user enterprise.

________________________________________

FUTURE-READY ARCHITECTURE

Design the system with extension points for:

•	CRM integrations with third-party systems (via REST APIs and webhooks) 

•	MLS/property feed imports and exports 

•	Mortgage broker integrations 

•	Conveyancer workflows 

•	Electronic document signing 

•	Digital identity verification 

•	Comparable sales database 

•	Property valuation tools 

•	AI-powered market insights 

•	Multi-country support (currencies, tax settings, address formats) 

________________________________________

Build the CRM around an "Inventory Command Centre" instead of just a list of properties.

The first screen an agency principal should sees each morning:

•	487 Active Mandates 

•	12 Awaiting Photos 

•	18 Mandates Expiring in 30 Days 

•	43 Buyer Requests With No Match 

•	27 Listings Missing Brochures 

•	15 New Leads Awaiting Response 

•	9 Price Reductions Recommended 

•	21 Properties Not Shared in the Last 14 Days 

Instead of simply storing information, the system actively tells the agency what needs attention next.

That shifts QuickProp Office from being a passive database into an operational tool that helps agencies market their full inventory more effectively and respond faster to buyers—a direction that aligns well with the overall strategy you've been developing.

This syncs seamlessly with quickprop agent and shares the same backend
