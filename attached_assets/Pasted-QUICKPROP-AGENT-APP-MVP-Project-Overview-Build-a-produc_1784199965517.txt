QUICKPROP AGENT APP (MVP)
Project Overview
Build a production-ready native mobile application called QuickProp Agent.
This application is designed for licensed estate agents and agency administrators to manage their property inventory while in the field.
The app must be extremely fast, intuitive and mobile-first.
The objective is:
Allow an agent to obtain a signed mandate and publish a professional property listing in under 5 minutes.
The app should feel closer to Airbnb Host than Salesforce.
Avoid unnecessary complexity.

SUCCESS CRITERIA
The app should allow an agent to:
• Create a professional listing in under 5 minutes.
• Instantly search every property within their agency.
• Generate a brochure with one tap.
• Share a property or their full catalogue directly using the native share sheet.
• Manage leads and buyer matches while in the field.
The app should feel like the indispensable daily tool for an estate agent—not just another portal app.

1. Quick Capture Mode
When an agent is standing at a property, they often don't have time to complete every field.
Add a Quick Capture option:
•	Take photos 
•	Record a short video 
•	Enter price 
•	Select suburb 
•	Save as Draft 
Everything uploads immediately, and the agent (or an office administrator) can complete the remaining details later. This reduces friction at the moment the mandate is secured.
2. Voice-to-Listing
Allow the agent to tap a microphone and say:
"Four-bedroom house in Borrowdale, double storey, borehole, solar, asking four hundred and twenty thousand dollars, approximately four thousand square metres..."
The app extracts structured fields and drafts the description automatically for review. That would dramatically reduce typing in the field and align with your goal of making listing creation as fast as possible.
